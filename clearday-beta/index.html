Currency and backups

Each currency uses a separate budget. Select a new currency to start a fresh budget, or return here to see your previous entries. No exchange rates or currency conversions are applied.

ClearDay never requests your bank login. Your entries are stored in this browser on this device. Clearing browser data can erase them, so make backups.

Important to know

Your daily figure is only a calculation based on information you enter. It does not verify your bank balance, automatically detect purchases, reserve real money, make payments or guarantee you can afford a transaction.
