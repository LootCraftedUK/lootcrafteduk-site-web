Can you try ClearDay for five minutes?

We are testing whether ClearDay is easy to understand. Please use made-up money figures and do not connect any accounts.

  1. Open the app and select Try a sample.
  2. Read the daily spending guide. What do you think the number means?
  3. Add a sample bill that repeats every 4 weeks.
  4. Add a sample income source that repeats every 4 weeks.
  5. Record a sample purchase, try Undo, and use the purchase preview.
