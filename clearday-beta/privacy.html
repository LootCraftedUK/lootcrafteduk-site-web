ClearDay private beta

Your information stays on your device

This beta uses no banking connection or account registration.

What is stored?

Sample budgets, bill names, payments, income information and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on the device where you use ClearDay. Anyone who can access that unlocked browser profile could see it. Browser data may be cleared by you or your device, and it is not automatically backed up.

What is sent to us?

ClearDay's budgeting interface does not intentionally send budget, balance, income or transaction entries to us or to an analytics provider. The hosting provider necessarily processes basic website access information such as requests and IP addresses in order to deliver the site and maintain its service; its handling of that information is governed by its own policies.

Feedback

We ask for voluntary usability comments only. Do not include personal financial details. The feedback worksheet stays on your device until you intentionally copy it and share it in the community where you found this test.

Be careful

This is a prototype and not financial advice. It may be wrong if bills or purchases were missed. Please test with made-up figures, and do not treat the daily guide as a guarantee that you can afford a purchase.

ClearDay beta currently has no tracking pixels, advertisements, bank login, paid subscriptions or cloud sync. You can delete browser storage to erase locally stored entries.
